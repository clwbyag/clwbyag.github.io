Workers' Concerns and the Future of Employment

Featured story · Labour

Behind every locomotive leaving Chittaranjan is a workforce whose lives are structured by roster, grade, housing and the promise that public railway employment still means something durable.

Security

Interviewees express anxiety about long-term employment, contractualisation and whether younger workers will inherit the same stability. These concerns should be paired with official workforce figures when available — see the Data Story.

Skills

If production becomes more assembly-oriented, workers fear skill atrophy: fewer opportunities to practise complex fabrication and diagnostics. Skills are not only personal assets; they are public industrial capability.

Township life

CLW is a works and a community. Changes in employment reverberate through schools, markets and local identity. Photo documentation of surroundings in the Photo Story supports this social context.
