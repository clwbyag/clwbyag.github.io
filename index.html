Introduction

Where India's locomotives were built

For decades, Chittaranjan Locomotive Works has stood as one of Indian Railways' most significant manufacturing establishments — a township forged around steel, skill and public-sector production.

Today, workers, union representatives and observers describe a shift in how locomotives are made: less complete indigenous manufacturing, more reliance on outside supply chains and assembly. Management voices emphasise modernisation, efficiency and continuity of production. Between these accounts lie unanswered questions about privatisation, ownership of critical processes, and the future of employment at CLW.

This website does not treat privatisation as a settled fact. It presents verified context where available, documents allegations and concerns raised by workers, records official statements, and marks what remains unconfirmed.

How to read this project

  • Verified facts — historically established or supported by official records.
  • Workers' accounts — allegations, experiences and concerns from the shop floor.
  • Official statements — management and public communications.
  • Open questions — claims that require further documentation.
