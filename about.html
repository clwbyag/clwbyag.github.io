About the Project

An academic multimedia journalism inquiry into CLW’s contested present.

Project background

CLW: The Changing Tracks

This website is an academic multimedia journalism project exploring concerns surrounding privatisation and the changing production structure of Chittaranjan Locomotive Works (CLW), Chittaranjan, West Bengal.

It is designed as an independent digital journalism presentation — not a corporate brochure. The goal is to inform, document and question, while maintaining clear boundaries between verified facts, workers’ allegations, official statements and unanswered questions.

Journalistic objectives

Explain CLW’s historical importance; document reported changes in manufacturing; surface workers’ and unions’ concerns; record management perspectives; and invite audiences to weigh incomplete public information without sensationalism.

Reporting methodology

Field observation, recorded interviews, photographic documentation, audio capture, and secondary research from official and archival sources. Sensitive claims are attributed. Privatisation is not asserted as fact without evidence.

Field visits & interviews

Interviews include labour union office-bearers (General Secretary, Secretary, President, Organising Secretary), CLW Deputy General Manager, Public Relations Officer, and workers. Add visit dates and locations when finalising the academic credit page.

Multimedia documentation

Photo essay, interview audio with transcripts, documentary video slot, and data visualisations prepared for verified statistics. Media folders are structured for easy replacement without redesign.

Research and sources

Primary sources: on-site interviews and observations. Secondary sources: Indian Railways / CLW publications, news archives, academic literature on public-sector manufacturing, and documents obtained through lawful access (including RTI where used). List full citations in your final submission bibliography.

Neutrality statement

This project does not campaign for or against privatisation as a policy slogan. It investigates what stakeholders say is happening to production and employment at CLW, and what remains unproven. Readers are encouraged to treat worker testimony and official messaging as distinct evidentiary categories.
